Hot. They came to you. Enjoy it. It is rare.

Qualifying in the pizza line

The two-minute conversation is an eval.

Do they ask real questions, or just wait for their turn to pitch?

Are they generous by default? Intros, ideas, attention.

Do they light up about anything at all?

Would I stand in this pizza line with them again?

I keep a ranking system with evals I tune every week. One of the fields is literally called cool. It has not been wrong yet.

Yes, this methodology has required a research quantity of pizza.
